Independent Testing

Peptide Testing Laboratories in the United States

Independent laboratories in the United States that test research peptides, compiled from lab sites, published certificates of analysis, and independent comparisons checked October–November 2026. Prices are as published and change without notice — confirm with the lab before shipping a sample. “Not found” means no evidence was found in the sources checked, not proof none exists.

Accreditation scope matters: no laboratory listed here has peptide purity or identity inside a published ISO/IEC 17025 accredited scope. ILS and Vanguard hold accreditation for other methods, and the PJLA scope supplement for Ethos Analytics (certificate L26-225, read November 14, 2026) lists no peptide methods. An accreditation badge alone does not mean a lab’s peptide work is accredited.
